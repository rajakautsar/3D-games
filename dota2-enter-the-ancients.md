## 🌌 About DOTA 2

DOTA 2 is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game developed and published by **Valve Corporation**. Two teams of five players compete to destroy the opposing team's "Ancient," a large structure located within their base.

### Key Features

- 🗺 **Massive Map** — A symmetrical battlefield divided into three lanes and a dense jungle
- 🦸 **124+ Heroes** — Each with unique abilities, roles, and playstyles
- 🏆 **Competitive Scene** — Home to **The International**, the world's largest esports prize pool
- 🆓 **Free to Play** — Full game available at no cost on Steam
- 🔄 **Constant Updates** — Regular patches, new heroes, and seasonal events

### Game Modes

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **All Pick** | Players select any available hero |
| **Ranked** | Competitive matchmaking with MMR |
| **Turbo** | Faster matches with accelerated gold |
| **Ability Draft** | Heroes draft abilities from random pools |
| **Arcade** | Custom community-made game modes |
